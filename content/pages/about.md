## The journey

She started on the other side of the speakers, presenting on air at Audwax and DYR. Radio taught her how audiences listen, what holds their attention and how a voice builds trust over time. Those lessons followed her into everything that came next.

In 2003 she began managing artists, and she has not stopped. That work made her fluent in the parts of the industry most people never see: contracts, bookings, promoters, tour logistics and the patience it takes to build a career instead of a moment.

## Marketing and communications

Alongside the music she built a parallel career in marketing and communications. She shapes how brands, artists and organisations are positioned, what they say and where they say it. Her strength is translation: helping corporate partners understand culture, and helping creatives speak the language of business.

## Entertainment and artist management

Her roster has included DJ Ganyani, DJ Josi Chave and Ismail Abrahams, as well as actors and actresses. She knows the rooms, the promoters and the paperwork, and she treats every artist as a business with a future, not a booking with a fee.

Behind the decks and on the mic she is DJ Bhoza and an MC who can hold a festival crowd or a black-tie dinner with equal ease.

## Creative industry involvement

She holds a music industry qualification from Wits University and is a partner at the Female DJ Network. She curates her own experiences, among them Soul Connex, Gospel Brunch and The Ultimate Camping Experience, and she shows up for the industry on panels, in workshops and in the green room.

## Leadership and advocacy

People call her the Creative Industry Big Sista for a reason. She mentors, opens doors and speaks plainly about the things that hold creatives back, from unfair contracts to the lack of visibility for women in music. Her work with Lawyers for the Arts South Africa and Rise Limpopo puts that advocacy into structure.

## Entrepreneurship

Every role she holds is also a business. Her events, her consultancy, her management work and The Bhoza Lifestyle collection are built to last, and built to make room for the people coming up behind her.
